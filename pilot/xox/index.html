Tic-Tac-Toe

🎮 Play classic Tic-Tac-Toe against the AI!
📜 How to play:

  • You are X, AI is O
  • Click an empty tile to place your X
  • Get 3 in a row (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal) to win
  • AI takes its turn after you
🏆 First to win ends the game, or it’s a draw if all tiles are filled!
