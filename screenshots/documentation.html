Canva Playstore Listing Template

About Canva Software

Canva is a user-friendly graphic design platform that allows anyone to create stunning visuals without needing advanced design skills. It's web-based, so you can access it from any browser, and it offers a wide range of templates, images, and tools for customization. With Canva, you can easily edit designs collaboratively or individually, making it perfect for creating app store screenshots and other marketing materials.

How to Use This Template

This template is designed for Play Store screenshot listings, featuring 8 mobile screens with sleek mobile frames and customizable heading text (typo). It's fully customizable, allowing you to simply drag and drop your own screenshots onto the mobile frames and edit the text to fit your app's branding.

To get started:

  • Open the template URL directly in Canva (no premium subscription required the free version is sufficient).
  • Drag and drop your app screenshots into the mobile frames provided in the template.
  • Edit the heading text (typo) by clicking on it and typing your desired content.
  • Customize any other elements as needed everything is editable.
  • Once you're satisfied, export the design in high-quality formats like PNG or JPG.

All images included in the template are high quality, ensuring your final output looks professional and polished on the Play Store.
