Reef Runner Documentation

About Reef Runner

One-Line Description: Guide a fish through a vibrant coral sea, dodging rocks in this fast-paced endless runner with intuitive controls and a thrilling vignette effect.

Full Description: Dive into Reef Runner, an exhilarating underwater adventure that tests your reflexes and agility! Take control of a lively fish navigating a spherical, ever-rolling coral sea filled with treacherous rocks. Your goal? Swim through three lanes, jumping and weaving to avoid obstacles in an endless race for survival. With a vibrant oceanic backdrop, smooth zig-zag animations, and a striking red vignette effect that pulses on every collision, the game immerses you in a dynamic underwater world. Optimized for both mobile and desktop, Reef Runner features intuitive swipe and keyboard controls, making it easy to pick up but hard to master. The game’s stunning visuals, powered by Three.js, include a shimmering sea, scattered clouds, and soft shadows, creating a captivating experience. Whether you’re dodging rocks or chasing high scores, Reef Runner offers endless fun for casual gamers and thrill-seekers alike. Swim fast, stay sharp, and conquer the reef!

How to Play

In Reef Runner, you control a fish swimming through a coral sea, avoiding rocks that appear in three lanes. Use quick reflexes to switch lanes or jump over obstacles.

Controls

  • Mobile:
    • Swipe Left/Right: Switch lanes.
    • Swipe Up: Jump to avoid rocks.
  • Desktop:
    • Left Arrow: Move to the left lane.
    • Right Arrow: Move to the right lane.
    • Up Arrow: Jump to avoid rocks.

Gameplay Tips

  • Watch for rocks spawning in one or two of the three lanes.
  • Time your jumps carefully to clear obstacles without colliding.
  • A red vignette effect flashes on collisions, signaling a hit.
  • Stay alert as the sea scrolls faster over time!

Technical Details

  • Platform: Web-based (mobile and desktop browsers).
  • Technology: Built with Three.js (r86) for 3D rendering, featuring a spherical sea, animated fish model, and dynamic lighting.
  • Assets: Includes a 3D fish model (FBX) with zig-zag swim animation and a custom texture.
  • Features: Endless runner mechanics, lane-based movement, jump mechanics, collision detection with vignette effect, and responsive design.

System Requirements

  • Browser: Chrome, Firefox, or any WebGL-supported browser.
  • Device: Mobile with touch support or desktop.
  • Internet: Required for initial asset loading (CDN scripts).
