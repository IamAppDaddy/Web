Speedometer App

Introducing Speedometer

Experience speed monitoring like never before with Speedometer, your reliable companion for real-time speed tracking. Designed with privacy and simplicity at its core, this app requires only location permission to function. No personal data is collected or shared, ensuring your privacy is always protected.

Perfect for driving, biking, or just satisfying your curiosity about speed, Speedometer delivers a distraction-free, reliable experience.

Key Features

  • Real-Time Speed Tracking
    Accurate, real-time speed data powered by your device’s location.
  • Privacy First
    No personal data is collected or shared, ever.
  • Ad-Free Experience
    Seamless and uninterrupted, with no ads.
  • Intuitive Design
    Simple, elegant interface for effortless use.

Extend the Possibilities

This open-source app is a foundation for your creativity. Explore how you can build on Speedometer’s code for your own projects.

Project Idea Description
Unit Conversions Add km/h to mph, knots, or m/s conversions for global users or niche activities like aviation.

Fitness Integration Sync with fitness apps to track speed, distance, or calories during workouts or cycling.
Vehicle Dashboard Create a full dashboard with GPS navigation, fuel monitoring, or speed limit alerts.
Drone Controller Track speed for drones or RC vehicles, with real-time data logging for analysis.
AR Overlay Use AR to display speed over real-world visuals, ideal for sports or adventure apps.
