Makkah Qibla Finder

Never miss your prayers again, no matter where you are! This app helps Muslims worldwide find the accurate Qibla direction for Salah (prayer) with ease and precision. It includes prayer timing notifications, an Islamic songs list with music player, and alarm reminders for prayer times.

Key Features

  • Prayer Timing with Notifications: Get notified before prayer times using the alarm manager.
  • Accurate Qibla Direction: Use advanced compass integration to find the Qibla direction with pinpoint accuracy based on user location.
  • Islamic Songs List with Music Player: Enjoy a curated list of Islamic songs with built-in music player functionality.
  • User-Friendly Interface: Simple, intuitive design makes it easy for anyone to use.
  • Works Everywhere: Whether at home, in the office, or traveling, the app ensures you can always find the right direction.
  • GPS and Compass Integration: Seamlessly combines GPS data with compass for accurate results.
  • Offline Mode: Find the Qibla direction even without an internet connection.
  • Customizable Settings: Adjust the app to suit your needs with various options.

How to Use the App

  1. Open the Makkah Qibla Finder app.
  2. Allow access to your location for precise results.
  3. Set your prayer times using the alarm manager for reminders.
  4. Follow the on-screen compass to find the direction of the Kaaba in Makkah.
  5. Browse the Islamic songs list and use the music player to listen.
  6. Begin your prayer with confidence, knowing you are facing the right direction.

This app is perfect for travelers, new Muslims, and anyone needing to quickly and accurately determine the Qibla direction.
